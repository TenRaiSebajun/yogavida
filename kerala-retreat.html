The Invitation

A Journey of Restoration

Omesh and Shannel invite you to join them for an unforgettable Ayurvedic Yoga Retreat in Kerala, India. Our mission is to guide you through a week of holistic rejuvenation — Ayurvedic therapies, daily yoga, meditation, nourishing food, and the gentle rhythm of life on the Malabar Coast.

What Awaits You

The Retreat Experience

The Venue

Manaltheeram Ayurveda Beach Village

Nestled on the Malabar Coast at the southern tip of India, Manaltheeram is a haven of traditional Ayurvedic healing surrounded by coconut palms and the Arabian Sea. Fly into Thiruvananthapuram (TRV) Airport — just a 40-minute transfer to the resort.

Nourishment

Ayurvedic Cuisine

All meals are freshly prepared, vegetarian and sattvic — designed to nourish the body, support digestion and complement your Ayurvedic treatments. The food is gentle, flavourful and sourced locally. Special dietary needs are catered for.

Healing

Daily Ayurvedic Treatments

Each guest receives daily authentic Ayurvedic treatments lasting 90 minutes to 2 hours, often administered by two therapists simultaneously. Your programme is personalised after a consultation with the resort's Ayurvedic doctor using organic medicinal plants grown in the resort's own herbal garden.

Practice

Yoga & Meditation

Morning and evening sessions led by Omesh and Shannel, suitable for all levels. From dynamic asana to restorative yin, pranayama to guided meditation — each day is thoughtfully sequenced to deepen your practice and support your healing journey.

Led By

Your Hosts

Yoga Vida Wellness

Shannel Jani

Multi-disciplined yoga practitioner of 18+ years, trained in Goa, India. Shannel teaches Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Yin and Restorative yoga, as well as meditation, pranayama and Ayurvedic wellness. Her warm, nurturing approach creates a safe space for every student.

True Roots Yoga

Omesh

Co-host from True Roots Yoga, Omesh brings deep knowledge of traditional yoga philosophy and practice. Together with Shannel, they create a balanced and enriching retreat experience, combining their complementary teaching styles and expertise.

Your Stay

Accommodation

Choose from five room types, each offering traditional Kerala architecture with modern comforts. All rooms include en-suite bathroom, air conditioning and free wi-fi.

Garden View

Standard Room

Approximately 18m², set within the resort's lush gardens. A peaceful, comfortable base with everything you need for a restorative stay.

Garden View

Garden Cottage

Approximately 20–25m² with beautiful garden views and a private terrace. A step up in space and privacy while surrounded by tropical greenery.

Partial Sea View

Special Cottage

Approximately 22–25m² with partial sea views and a hammock on your private veranda. The perfect balance of comfort and coastal charm.

Full Sea View

Deluxe Cottage

Approximately 22–25m² with unobstructed sea views. Wake up to the sound of waves and enjoy the most stunning panoramas the resort has to offer.

Partial Sea View · Heritage

Kerala House Deluxe

Approximately 30m² in a traditional heritage wooden house with partial sea views. The most spacious and characterful accommodation option, steeped in Kerala's architectural tradition.
